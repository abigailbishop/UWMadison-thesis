\chapter{Askaryan Radio Array}

To have a good chance at observing UHE neutrinos, we need large volumes of dielectric material. 
Sand and salt are viable dielectric materials -- sand being plentiful in deserts and beaches while large salt depositions are scattered across the world -- but ice is a great candidate for a UHE neutrino search. 
Our world has multiple glaciers with kilometers of depth and millions of square kilometers of surface area. 
Some glaciers already have infrastructure to support research experiments as well, like Summit Station in Greenland and Amundsen-Scott South Pole Station in Antarctica.
